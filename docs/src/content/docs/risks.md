---
title: Risks
description: What can go wrong.
---

Trading perpetual futures can lose money, including your whole deposit.

- **Copy trading:** you take on the trader's losses.
- **Delta-Neutral:** funding can turn negative, and a leg can be liquidated. If one leg is closed, stopped or liquidated, the hedge guard closes the other leg and notifies you.
- **Venues:** a venue can be down, change its fees, or restrict your region. Check each venue before you sign up.
- **One account, one Mimiq:** while Mimiq holds a position on a venue account, do not trade that market on the venue's own screen, from another bot, or from a second Mimiq wallet linked to the same account. Mimiq measures its orders on the account's position and may take the other trade's move for its own or close it with its own.
- **Mimiq** is software. It can have bugs and outages.

Nothing here is financial advice. Mimiq makes no promise of returns.
